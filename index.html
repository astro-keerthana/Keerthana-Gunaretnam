About

About Me

I work where computational biology, disease omics and space biology meet. My research uses RNA expression profiling and in silico study design to find shared molecular signatures across inflammatory and neurodegenerative disease, and extends into astrobiology through work on lunar regolith, astromicrobes, biosignatures and microbial adaptation in space-relevant environments. I also research physiology and hardware interfacing for bio-intelligence systems. I enjoy ambitious problems, and my strength is pairing careful analysis with the communication needed to carry it across disciplines.
