About

About Me

I work at the meeting point of computational biology, disease omics, and space biology. My research uses RNA expression profiling and in silico study design to find shared molecular signatures across inflammatory and neurodegenerative disease, and extends into astrobiology through work on lunar regolith, astromicrobes, biosignatures, and microbial adaptation in space-relevant environments. Alongside that, I research physiology and hardware interfacing for bio-intelligence systems. I thrive on challenge and ambitious goals, and my strength is combining analytical work with the communication needed to carry it across disciplines.
